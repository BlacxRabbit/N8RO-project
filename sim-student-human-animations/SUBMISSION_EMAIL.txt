Brief description:
My project implements a custom N8RO animation plugin for a Nathan human GLB character. The model cycles through three motion states: Right Arm Wave, Walk In Place, and Both Arms Raise. These motions are driven by my custom DLL animation model and triggered by the included Lua mission script, not by the default idle animation.

The model controls these 10 joints:
1. rightShoulder
2. rightElbow
3. leftShoulder
4. leftElbow
5. leftHip
6. rightHip
7. leftKnee
8. rightKnee
9. leftAnkle
10. rightAnkle
